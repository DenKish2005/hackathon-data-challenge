# Freedom logo asset

`freedom-logo.png` is the existing transparent Freedom F-shield mark, used as requested by the user. Source: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/nvstly/icons/main/ticker_icons/FRHC.png (NVSTly ticker icon collection, FRHC / Freedom Holding).

The asset is stored locally; the app makes no external image request. It is not an AI-generated approximation. The adjacent FREEDOM / BROKER text is typeset in the interface. The mark belongs to its respective owner. This source is a public icon collection, not an official brandbook; no assertion of official typography or approved brand tokens is made.
